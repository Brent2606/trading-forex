      Paper account
      
              
              
              
              
              
      Stop-out closes the biggest loser when margin level falls below 50%. A margin-call warning shows below 100%. By default the simulator follows real FX hours: closed from Friday 17:00 to Sunday 17:00 New York time.
      
              
      
                
                
                  Wipe positions and history?
                  
                  
                
              
      
            
      
            
      
              
      Rules that keep accounts alive
      
              
        
                
      1. Risk 0.5–1% per trade. Ten straight losses then cost about 10%, not the account.
        2. 
                
      2. Decide the stop before the entry. Size comes from the stop, not the other way around.
        3. 
                
      3. Aim for at least 1.5R. A 40% win rate at 2R is profitable; 60% at 0.5R is not.
        4. 
                
      4. Correlated pairs are one bet. Long EUR/USD and long GBP/USD doubles your USD exposure.
        5. 
                
      5. Spreads widen at the 17:00 New York rollover and on news. Avoid tight stops then.
        6. 
                
      6. Backtest on real data, then paper trade, then go small. Results here are simulated.
